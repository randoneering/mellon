# Mellon documentation guidance

This original guidance applies the public writing requirements in the repository's `AGENTS.md` to worker-authored documentation. The repository's existing `LICENSE` (GNU AGPL version 3) applies to this file.

- Use active voice and present tense.
- Keep sections short and use direct bullets.
- Prefer concrete numbers and observable behavior over vague claims.
- Avoid filler, buzzwords, and claims that imply certainty beyond the evidence.
- Describe the classifier as a fallible signal, not proof of AI authorship.
- State what was tested and identify behavior that remains unverified.
- Write one sentence per Markdown source line.
- Keep comments focused on why a decision exists, not what code does.
- Make errors specific and actionable without exposing credentials, private text, or raw service responses.
- Do not copy local harness instructions or ignored OpenSpec content into project documentation.
